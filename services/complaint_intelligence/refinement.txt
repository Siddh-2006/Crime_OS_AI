The current Complaint Intelligence pipeline processes the complaint and all attached evidence together and stores the original complaint, processed complaint, evidence intelligence, timeline intelligence, and investigation intelligence inside the complaint document. While this works during complaint registration, it is not suitable for real-world investigations where additional evidence can be submitted at any point during the lifecycle of a case. I want to refactor the architecture to support incremental evidence processing. A complaint should be processed exactly once, producing an immutable ComplaintProfile. Every evidence item (image, video, audio, PDF, document, etc.) should become an independent entity with its own lifecycle and should be processed exactly once by its corresponding worker, producing a permanent EvidenceProfile. Previously processed evidence must never be processed again unless explicitly requested. Whenever a new evidence file is uploaded, only that evidence should pass through the appropriate worker (Image, Video, Audio, PDF, etc.), generate its EvidenceProfile, and then trigger the downstream reasoning pipeline consisting of Intelligence Fusion → Timeline Engine → Timeline Intelligence → Investigation Intelligence. The Complaint Worker must not execute again, and existing Evidence Workers must not execute again. The intelligence layer should always consume the stored ComplaintProfile together with all existing EvidenceProfiles to generate an updated, living Case Intelligence that evolves as new evidence is added. Separate the responsibilities so that the Complaint remains the original immutable record, each Evidence has its own processed representation, and the Case Intelligence becomes the continuously updated semantic understanding of the investigation.
